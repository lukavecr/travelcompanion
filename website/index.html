Features

Everything a long trip needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Your data lives on your phone first, so the app keeps working in the metro, on a plane or in the mountains.

Day-by-day plan

Trips, days and stops in order. Stays from check-in to check-out, times and reminders before each event.

Tickets & documents

Take a photo of a boarding pass or ticket. The app reads the QR code and text and fills in flight, seat and gate. Show the code full screen at the gate.

QR · IATA BCBP · Latin & Japanese text

Money

Log expenses in any currency, converted to your home currency with daily ECB rates. Scan receipts, see spending by day, category and country.

Places & map

Share a place from Google Maps straight into your trip. See all your places on an OpenStreetMap map, with your own position.

Travel Vault

Passport, visas, insurance, vaccinations and emergency contacts. End-to-end encrypted, unlocked with your fingerprint. Reminds you before a document expires.

AES-256-GCM

SOS

One tap sends a text with your location to your emergency contacts. A card in English shows first responders your medical details.

How it works

From a crumpled ticket to a plan in seconds.

Text and code recognition runs on your phone. Your photos are not sent anywhere to be read.

  1. Snap the ticket

    Boarding pass, train ticket, hotel booking or a screenshot.

  2. Check what was read

    Flight number, booking reference, date, time, seat and gate are filled in. Fix anything with one tap.

  3. It lands in your plan

    The ticket appears on the right day with a reminder. At the gate, open the code full screen.

Privacy

Your passport is nobody else's business.

No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Read the privacy policy.

  • Encrypted on the phone. The app's database is encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore.
  • App lock. Fingerprint, face or phone PIN when you open the app.
  • Vault encrypted end to end. Only you can read it, even in the cloud backup.
  • Reading happens on the device. Ticket and receipt photos are processed locally.
  • You can delete everything. All data or your whole account, right in the app.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Trips, plans, documents, codes, expenses and the Vault are stored on your phone. Exchange rates, place search and the map need a connection.

What happens if I lose my phone?

With Pro, your data is backed up to the cloud and comes back when you sign in on a new phone. The Vault is restored with your Vault password.

Is there an iPhone version?

It is in progress. Android comes first.

I don't get reminders on my Xiaomi phone.

Some phones (Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO and others) stop apps in the background to save battery. Open Settings → Apps → Travel Companion → Battery saver and choose “No restrictions”.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Details are on the account deletion page.
