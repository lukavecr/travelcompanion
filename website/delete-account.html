Delete your account

You can delete your Comes Viae account and all data connected to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Comes Viae and go to Settings (gear icon on the Trips tab).
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your fingerprint or phone PIN and your account password.

What gets deleted

  • your account (e-mail and password),
  • the whole cloud backup: trips, places, documents, tickets, expenses and images,
  • the encrypted Travel Vault backup and its key,
  • the data on your phone.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. An active Pro subscription is not cancelled automatically: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write from the e-mail address of your account to lrtechcz@gmail.com with the subject “Delete account”. We will delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.
