Privacy policy

Comes Viae (“the app”, “we”) is a travel planning app for Android, developed by an independent developer, LR Tech CZ, Czech Republic. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored and what your rights are. In short: your data is yours, it lives on your phone first, and we do not sell it, show ads or track you.

Contact for anything about privacy: lrtechcz@gmail.com

1. Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter is stored in a database on your phone. The database is encrypted; its key is kept in the Android Keystore. This includes:

  • trips, day plans, places and notes,
  • documents and tickets (photos, recognised text, QR and barcode content, flight details, passenger name, seat),
  • expenses and receipt photos,
  • Travel Vault items (passport, visas, insurance, vaccinations, emergency contacts, medical details), which are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Photos of documents and receipts are stored in the app’s private storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. More in Security.

2. Account

To use cloud backup you create an account with your e-mail address and a password. Accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We use the e-mail address only to sign you in and, if you write to us, to reply.

3. Cloud backup (Pro)

If you have Comes Viae Pro and are signed in, the app backs up your data to Google Cloud Firestore so you can restore it on another phone. The backup contains your trips, places, documents, tickets, expenses and reduced-size images. It is stored in your own area of the database, which only your signed-in account can access.

  • The whole backup is end-to-end encrypted on your phone before upload. Its key is protected by your Vault password, so nobody else, including Google and us, can read it.
  • Travel Vault is end-to-end encrypted. Vault items and photos leave your phone only in encrypted form. The key is protected by your Vault password (PBKDF2-SHA256), which we never receive. Without that password, nobody, including us, can read the Vault.

Free users' data stays only on their phone.

4. Processing on your device

Reading text, QR codes and barcodes from photos is done on your phone with Google ML Kit. Photos are not uploaded for recognition. ML Kit may contact Google to download model updates and may send anonymous performance and usage metrics to Google.

5. Location

The app asks for location permission only when you use it: to show your position on the map, to save your current spot as a place, to include your location in an SOS message, or to record a track. A track is recorded only after you press Start (in the app or on the widget) and until you press Stop; while it runs, a notification is shown. Track points are stored on your phone (and in your backup, if you use it). Your location is never sent to us. An SOS message is sent by your phone’s own messaging app to the contacts you chose.

Steps. If you turn on steps on the home screen widget, the app reads your phone’s step counter (permission “Physical activity”). The number is shown only on the widget and is not stored or sent anywhere.

7. Notifications

Reminders (events, check-ins, expiring documents) are scheduled locally on your phone. Reminder texts for documents do not contain the document's details.

8. How long we keep data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud backup stays until you delete it in the app or delete your account. Deleted items are removed from the backup when the app syncs.

9. Deleting your data

  • All data: Settings → Delete all data. Deletes the cloud backup first, then the data on your phone.
  • Account: Settings → Delete account. Deletes your account, cloud backup and Vault backup. See the account deletion page.
  • If you cannot use the app, write to us and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. In the Czech Republic that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ, uoou.gov.cz). The legal basis for processing is the performance of our agreement with you (providing the app and the backup you asked for).

11. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top. Important changes will also be announced in the app.
