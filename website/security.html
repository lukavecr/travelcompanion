Security

Your passport is nobody else’s business.

Comes Viae holds some of the most sensitive things you carry: your passport, visas, insurance, tickets and where you are. Here is exactly how they are protected, in plain words, including what no app can protect you from.

1. On your phone

  • Encrypted database. All trips, plans, documents and expenses are stored in an AES-256 encrypted database. Its key is generated on your phone and kept in the Android Keystore.
  • Encrypted photos. Photos of tickets, boarding passes and receipts are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved.
  • App lock. Fingerprint, face or your phone PIN when you open the app and after a minute in the background.
  • Screen protection. Documents and the Vault cannot be captured in screenshots or screen recordings and are hidden in the recent apps overview.
  • No copies elsewhere. Android’s automatic system backup is turned off for the app, and temporary export files are deleted.

2. Travel Vault

  • Passport, visas, insurance, vaccinations and medical details are encrypted a second time with their own AES-256-GCM key, separate from the rest of the app.
  • The Vault locks together with the app. Expiry reminders work even while it is locked and never show the document number.
  • Its backup is end-to-end encrypted: the key leaves your phone only wrapped by your Vault password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations). We never receive the password, so nobody, including us, can read your Vault.

3. Cloud backup and shared trips

  • The backup (Pro) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in an area that only your signed-in account can open. These access rules are tested automatically.
  • The whole backup, including photos, is always end-to-end encrypted. Its key is protected by your Vault password, so nobody else, not Google and not us, can read it. Nothing is uploaded until encryption is set up.
  • Shared trips are encrypted with a key that only the members have. It is passed on in the invite code, never stored readable in the cloud. Documents, the Vault and your tracks are never shared.
  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS.

4. What stays on your phone

  • Reading tickets and receipts (text, QR and barcodes) happens on the phone. Photos are not uploaded anywhere to be read.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics. We do not sell or share your data.

5. What no app can protect you from

  • Someone holding your phone while it and the app are unlocked.
  • A phone with malware, a modified (rooted) system or no screen lock.
  • A forgotten Vault password: by design, nobody can reset it, so a Vault backup cannot be restored without it.

6. Our tips

  • Use a screen lock with fingerprint or face and keep your phone updated.
  • Choose a long, unique Vault password and keep it somewhere safe, for example in a password manager.
  • Never reuse your Vault password anywhere else.
